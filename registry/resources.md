# Citizen Laws — Registry of Free UK Help

- **StepChange Debt Charity:** Free online debt advice and plans. (https://www.stepchange.org)
- **National Debtline:** Phone and webchat debt advice. (https://www.nationaldebtline.org)
- **Citizens Advice:** General legal, debt, and consumer rights. (https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk)
- **Financial Ombudsman Service:** Escalate unresolved complaints. (https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk)
- **Shelter:** Housing debt and eviction defense. (https://www.shelter.org.uk)
- **Christians Against Poverty (CAP):** In-depth debt help. (https://capuk.org)
- **PayPlan:** Free debt management plans. (https://www.payplan.com)
