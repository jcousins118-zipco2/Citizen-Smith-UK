# Regulation: CONC 7.13.10
**What it means:** A lender cannot subject you to concurrent, repetitive contact by different parties (like a bank and their debt collection agency) for the same debt.
**What to do:** Cite this if you are being harassed by both the original lender and their collection agency simultaneously.

# Regulation: CONC 7.13.5
**What it means:** Lenders must maintain data integrity. They cannot send you contradictory messages (e.g., one department offering help while another sends default threats).
**What to do:** Use this if you are receiving mixed messages or incorrect information.

# Regulation: CONC 7.14.1
**What it means:** A firm must suspend enforcement action (like threatening default notices or account closure) if the debt is under an active, unresolved dispute.
**What to do:** Cite this if you've notified them of a dispute and they keep escalating.

# Regulation: CONC 7.3
**What it means:** Firms must treat vulnerable customers fairly and with forbearance.
**What to do:** Use this if you have documented health or personal issues (like your mother's condition) and they aren't adjusting their behavior.

# Regulation: CONC 8.x (Arrears)
**What it means:** Rules specifically governing how creditors must handle arrears, including being proportionate and allowing time to pay.
**What to do:** Use this to push for interest freezes or repayment holidays.
