## 5. About page

### Title
**About Citizen Smith**

Citizen Smith is a free rights-and-action platform for people under pressure.

It exists for people who are trying to deal with institutions while stressed, tired, vulnerable, low on confidence, or simply overwhelmed.

The goal is simple:
- help people understand what may apply
- help them send the right thing
- help them escalate properly if needed

Citizen Smith is not a law firm.
It does not replace professional advice where that is needed.
What it does is make the system easier to understand and easier to act on.

Everything is built around plain English, trusted sources, and practical next steps.

### About values
Citizen Smith is built on a few fixed principles:
- the user comes first
- rights guidance should be free
- clarity matters more than jargon
- vulnerable people deserve systems that make sense
- trust matters more than short-term money

## 7. Tone guide for site copy

Citizen Smith copy should be:
- calm
- direct
- plain English
- non-judgmental
- not patronising
- not alarmist
- steady under pressure

Avoid:
- sales language
- hype
- legal grandstanding
- corporate softness
- empty reassurance

Preferred feeling:
A smart, steady person helping you think clearly when things are messy.

## 9. Disclaimer starter copy

Citizen Smith provides information, document support, and guidance based on official and trusted sources.
It is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation.

Template wording may be used in emails, letters, webforms, live chat, or phone conversations. Where possible, users should create or keep a written record.

Before pasting text into any tool, remove names, addresses, account numbers, dates of birth, and other identifying details.

If your situation is urgent, severe, or already in court, you may need specialist advice alongside this site.
