# Citizen Smith — Document Pack 01 (Debt & Credit)

This pack contains the first five live user documents for V1.
Each one is designed to be copied, adapted, and sent by the user.

---

## 1. Dual-Contact Challenge

### Use when
- a creditor and debt collector are both contacting you about the same debt
- you do not know who is in control
- you are receiving overlapping demands or instructions

### Purpose
- stop overlapping contact
- force clarity on who owns or controls the debt
- force one named point of contact

### Likely rule
- FCA CONC 7.13.10

### Template

**Subject:** Formal request for one point of contact and confirmation of account control

I am writing regarding the above account because I am currently being contacted by more than one party about the same debt.

At present, this is causing confusion and distress. I do not know which organisation is in control of the account, who has authority to give instructions, or who I am expected to deal with.

Under FCA CONC 7.13.10 and the wider expectation of fair treatment, I should not be subjected to overlapping or repetitive contact by multiple parties regarding the same debt.

I require written confirmation of the following:

1. who currently owns or controls this debt
2. whether any third party is acting on your behalf, and with what authority
3. who my single point of contact is going forward
4. whether any other party has been instructed to stop contacting me directly

Until this is clarified, I require all parties to stop sending overlapping demands or conflicting instructions.

Please reply in writing only.

[NAME]
[EMAIL]
[REFERENCE IF NEEDED]

### Evidence to keep
- all letters/emails from both parties
- different balances or payment instructions
- dates/times of overlapping contact

### Next escalation
- formal complaint
- FOS if confusion or harm continues

## 2. Active Dispute / No-Default Warning

### Use when
- you already disputed the debt or lodged a complaint
- they are now threatening default, closure, or enforcement anyway

### Purpose
- restate the dispute clearly
- challenge the threat of escalation
- preserve a clean record for ombudsman escalation

### Likely rule
- FCA CONC 7.14.1

### Template

**Subject:** Active dispute — challenge to default or enforcement threat

I am writing to confirm that this account remains in active dispute.

I first raised this dispute on [DATE]. It has not been properly resolved.

Despite this, I have now received communication threatening [default / account closure / enforcement action].

Under FCA CONC 7.14.1, enforcement action should not continue while a genuine dispute remains unresolved. I therefore dispute the fairness and regulatory basis of this threat.

Please confirm in writing:

1. that the account is marked as disputed
2. that no default or enforcement step will be taken while the dispute remains unresolved
3. that I will receive a substantive written response to the dispute already raised

Please treat this as a formal complaint if you have not already done so.

I require written communication only.

[NAME]
[EMAIL]
[REFERENCE IF NEEDED]

### Evidence to keep
- first dispute email/letter
- any complaint reference
- later threat messages
- timeline of unanswered correspondence

### Next escalation
- formal complaint
- FOS
- credit-file correction if harm occurs later

## 3. Contradiction Challenge

### Use when
- one message offers help or says the matter is being handled
- another message escalates or threatens default
- the same creditor group is giving inconsistent instructions

### Purpose
- force a written explanation
- challenge administrative failure
- clarify the true account position

### Likely rule
- FCA CONC 7.13.5

### Template

**Subject:** Formal complaint about contradictory account communications

I am writing because I have received contradictory communications about the same account.

For example:
- on [DATE], I was told [SUMMARY OF HELP / HOLDING MESSAGE]
- on [DATE], I was then sent [SUMMARY OF THREAT / ESCALATION]

These messages are inconsistent and have caused confusion and distress.

Under FCA CONC 7.13.5 and the wider expectation of fair treatment, your records and communications should be accurate, consistent, and properly handled.

I require written clarification of:

1. the current true status of the account
2. whether the matter is under review, complaint, or collections handling
3. why contradictory messages have been sent
4. what steps you are taking to prevent further conflicting contact

Please treat this as a formal complaint if necessary.

I require written communication only.

[NAME]
[EMAIL]
[REFERENCE IF NEEDED]

### Evidence to keep
- copies/screenshots of conflicting messages
- dates and departments where shown
- any earlier promises later contradicted

### Next escalation
- formal complaint
- FOS if unresolved

## 4. Vulnerability Notice

### Use when
- you need the firm to understand hardship, illness, disability, bereavement, dyslexia, caring duties, mental distress, or other vulnerability
- you need adjustments or forbearance

### Purpose
- put vulnerability on record
- request specific adjustments
- seek fairer handling

### Likely rule
- FCA CONC 7.3

### Template

**Subject:** Vulnerability notice and request for adjusted handling

I am writing to place my circumstances on record and request that this account is handled appropriately.

I am currently affected by the following circumstances:

[BRIEF DESCRIPTION — for example: financial hardship, dyslexia, bereavement, caring responsibilities, illness, mental distress]

These circumstances are affecting my ability to deal with this matter in the usual way.

Under FCA CONC 7.3 and the wider expectation that vulnerable customers are treated fairly, I am asking you to make reasonable adjustments and consider appropriate forbearance.

I request the following:

1. written communication where possible, preferably by email
2. no unnecessary phone contact
3. consideration of a payment pause / interest freeze / temporary hold while I stabilise matters
4. confirmation that my circumstances have been recorded and will be taken into account

Please confirm in writing how you will handle this account going forward.

[NAME]
[EMAIL]
[REFERENCE IF NEEDED]

### Evidence to keep
- note of what you disclosed and when
- any replies ignoring the disclosure
- any specific adjustment requested

### Next escalation
- formal complaint for vulnerability mishandling
- FOS
- debt-advice referral where needed

## 5. Request for Written Communication Where Possible

### Use when
- phone calls are making things worse
- you need a written paper trail
- you have dyslexia, anxiety, cognitive load, or another reason for preferring written contact

### Purpose
- reduce pressure and confusion
- create a written record
- encourage communication in a format the user can manage more confidently

### Likely rule
- FCA fair treatment / vulnerability / proportionate communications principles

### Template

**Subject:** Request for written communication where possible

I am requesting that communication about this account is made in writing where possible, preferably by email.

Telephone contact is making this matter harder for me to manage, and I need a clear written record so I can properly understand and respond.

Please therefore:

1. use written communication wherever possible
2. avoid unnecessary phone contact
3. confirm in writing that this communication preference has been recorded

If there is any formal reason why a particular communication must be sent in another way, please make that clear.

If relevant, please also treat this as part of my vulnerability disclosure and account handling needs.

[NAME]
[EMAIL]
[REFERENCE IF NEEDED]

### Evidence to keep
- any prior request for written contact
- call logs after the request
- any written refusal or failure to comply

### Next escalation
- oppressive contact complaint
- vulnerability complaint
- FOS if conduct continues
