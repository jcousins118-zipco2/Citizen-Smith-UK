# Citizen Smith — Positioning

## 1. Category
Citizen Smith is a **free rights-and-action platform**.

It helps people:
- understand what is happening
- identify the likely rule or right that applies
- use the right document or wording
- escalate properly if they are ignored or mistreated

Citizen Smith is **not** positioned as an AI legal product.
It is **not** a law firm.
It is **not** a referral funnel.
It is **not** just a complaint generator.

---

## 2. Core Promise
**Know your rights. Send the right thing. Escalate properly.**

Longer form:
Citizen Smith helps people understand their rights, use the right document or wording, and escalate properly — using plain English, trusted sources, and step-by-step support.

---

## 3. Target User
Citizen Smith is designed for people who are:
- under pressure
- confused by institutions
- low-confidence with formal systems
- overwhelmed by jargon or complexity
- vulnerable, stressed, bereaved, financially strained, or cognitively overloaded

It should work for users who are intelligent but exhausted.
That is a central design assumption.

---

## 4. Problem We Solve
Most people do not fail because they have no rights.
They fail because:
- they do not know which right applies
- they do not know what to say
- they do not know what evidence matters
- they do not know when or where to escalate
- existing systems assume confidence, literacy, and emotional bandwidth they may not have

Citizen Smith closes that gap.

---

## 5. What Makes Citizen Smith Different

### A. Diagnosis → document → escalation
This is the core product chain.
Citizen Smith does not stop at information.
It helps the user move from:
- problem recognition
- to action
- to escalation

### B. Source-led, not invented
Citizen Smith is built on:
- official law and regulator sources
- trusted support organisations
- clear attribution and last-checked discipline

This is a major differentiator from generic AI legal tools.

### C. Built for vulnerable and low-confidence users
Citizen Smith treats low cognitive load as a product requirement, not a side issue.

### D. Multi-sector by design
Most existing organisations specialise in one area.
Citizen Smith is designed to grow across debt, housing, energy, telecoms, benefits, employment, and more.

### E. Free to the user
Citizen Smith is not a paywalled protection product.
The public-help layer remains free.

---

## 6. What Citizen Smith Is Not
Citizen Smith is not:
- a solicitor substitute
- a law firm
- an AI chatbot pretending to know law
- a legal referral funnel
- a generic letter template dump
- a paid “upload your problem and we’ll handle it” service

This matters because the market already contains many of these.
Citizen Smith should stay clearly separate from them.

---

## 7. Competitive Contrast

### Against generic AI legal tools
They sell conversation.
Citizen Smith sells clarity, sources, and next steps.

### Against complaint generators
They help people send something.
Citizen Smith helps people understand what applies, send the right thing, and escalate correctly.

### Against charities and support bodies
They are trusted but usually sector-specific and often overloaded.
Citizen Smith complements them by giving a structured entry point and routing layer.

### Against legal referral platforms
They route people toward lawyers.
Citizen Smith prioritises self-help and informed action first.

---

## 8. Launch Wedge
Citizen Smith should launch as:

**A free UK rights-and-action platform, starting with debt and credit.**

This gives:
- a sharp real-world entry point
- immediate usefulness
- a clear pain market
- strong need among vulnerable users
- natural expansion into adjacent sectors later

The launch story is narrow enough to build, but broad enough to signal the larger system.

---

## 9. Brand Position
Citizen Smith should feel:
- calm
- clear
- intelligent
- practical
- on the user’s side
- non-judgmental
- not theatrical
- not patronising

It should feel like a smart, steady ally who can help someone think straight under pressure.

---

## 10. Strategic Moat
The moat is not just templates.
The moat is the combination of:
- trusted source discipline
- issue diagnosis
- escalation logic
- low-cognitive-load design
- vulnerable-user fit
- cross-sector architecture

Any one of these exists elsewhere.
The combination does not.

---

## 11. Ethical Position
Citizen Smith is commercially allowed but ethically constrained.
It is free to the user.
It may earn from aligned advertisers and sponsors.
But revenue must never distort rights guidance.

The trust model is part of the positioning.
Being clearly on the citizen’s side is not branding fluff — it is product strategy.

---

## 12. Positioning Summary
Citizen Smith is a free, source-led, plain-English rights-and-action platform for people under pressure.

It helps users:
- understand what rule may apply
- use the right document or wording
- gather the right evidence
- escalate properly

It is designed especially for people who are vulnerable, overwhelmed, or not confident dealing with institutions.

That is the core position.
