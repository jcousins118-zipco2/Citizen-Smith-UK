Sources

Citizen Smith is built from a source-led model. We do not invent legal positions and we do not copy whole third-party guidance pages.

Primary sources first

  • FCA Handbook
  • GOV.UK
  • legislation.gov.uk
  • official ombudsman and regulator pages

Trusted support sources second

  • Citizens Advice
  • StepChange
  • National Debtline
  • MoneyHelper
  • Shelter and other approved support bodies where relevant

What we publish

We publish plain-English summaries, short quoted authority where useful, practical wording, evidence guidance, and escalation routes.

Each issue should be linked back to real sources and checked over time.
