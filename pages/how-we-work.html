How We Work

Citizen Smith is built to help people move from confusion to a practical next step.

The basic flow

  1. You choose who the problem is with.
  2. You choose what they are saying or doing.
  3. We show a likely rule or position that may help.
  4. We show wording you can use in writing or conversation.
  5. We show what evidence to keep and where to escalate next.

What we optimise for

  • plain English
  • low cognitive load
  • practical next steps
  • clear evidence trails
  • source-led guidance

What we avoid

  • legal jargon where it is not needed
  • pretending every case is certain
  • pushing people into unnecessary complexity
  • turning vulnerable users into sales leads
