# Citizen Smith — Ethical Commercial Model

## Core Position
Citizen Smith exists to help people protect themselves.
It is free to the user.
It is ethically commercial, not charity-only and not exploitative.

Profit is allowed.
Misalignment is not.

The model is simple:
- users do not pay
- guidance stays source-led and independent
- revenue comes only from appropriate advertisers, sponsors, and aligned partners
- commercial activity must never weaken the protection offered to the user

---

## 1. Non-Negotiable Behaviour Rules

### Rule 1 — The user is not the product
Citizen Smith must not monetise personal distress, panic, vulnerability, bereavement, confusion, disability, or hardship in a predatory way.

### Rule 2 — No paywall on protection
Core rights guidance, templates, escalation paths, and source references must remain free.

### Rule 3 — Editorial independence is absolute
No advertiser, sponsor, affiliate, or commercial partner may influence:
- legal summaries
- issue prioritisation
- template wording
- escalation guidance
- source selection
- warning language

### Rule 4 — Trust is the primary asset
If a revenue opportunity would reduce trust, it should be rejected.
Short-term money is not worth long-term damage.

### Rule 5 — Vulnerable users come first
Where there is tension between monetisation and protection, protection wins.

---

## 2. Revenue Model

Citizen Smith may earn from:

### A. Ethical advertising
Display placements or sponsor placements from businesses that are relevant, safe, and non-predatory.

### B. Sponsorship
Sponsored placement of guides, sections, or tools — only where sponsorship does not alter the guidance.

### C. Ethical affiliate / referral arrangements
Only where:
- the referred service is genuinely useful
- the service does not exploit vulnerable users
- the commercial relationship is clearly disclosed
- the recommendation would still be made without payment

### D. Grants and public-interest funding
Citizen Smith may seek grant support for financial resilience, accessibility, legal empowerment, and public-interest consumer protection.

### E. B2B / institutional products later
Training, frameworks, or support tools for charities, advisors, researchers, journalists, or accessibility-focused organisations may be commercialised later — provided the public-help layer remains free.

---

## 3. Acceptable Advertiser Categories

Examples of categories that may be acceptable, subject to review:

- budgeting and money-management tools
- savings and bill-reduction tools
- accessibility-support services
- mental health or carer-support services
- ethical financial education products
- consumer-rights support tools
- document and organisation tools
- credit-file monitoring or correction tools, if transparently priced and non-predatory
- comparison or switching services, if genuinely useful and fair
- selected insurance or utility-saving services, if appropriate to the user base

Acceptance always depends on conduct, not category label alone.

---

## 4. Banned Advertiser Categories

Citizen Smith must not accept advertising, sponsorship, or referral money from businesses that profit from distress, desperation, or confusion.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- payday lenders
- high-cost short-term credit providers
- predatory debt settlement firms
- aggressive IVA lead-generation businesses
- claims-farm operators
- misleading credit-repair services
- spammy lead brokers
- exploitative legal SEO businesses
- gambling
- misleading “instant cash” offers
- any service that pressures users while they are vulnerable

If a company’s business model depends on poor user understanding, it should not appear on Citizen Smith.

---

## 5. Editorial / Commercial Separation

Citizen Smith must operate with three clear walls:

### Editorial wall
Owns:
- rights guidance
- legal summaries
- issue trees
- source references
- templates
- escalation logic

This layer must remain independent.

### Commercial wall
Owns:
- sponsorships
- ad placements
- partner agreements
- media pack / revenue operations

This layer cannot edit or shape the rights content.

### Trust wall
Owns:
- advertiser standards
- conflict rules
- disclosure policy
- user protection rules
- banned categories

This wall exists to stop commercial creep.

---

## 6. Disclosure Rules

All paid relationships must be clearly disclosed.

Users must be able to tell:
- what is editorial
- what is sponsored
- what is an advertisement
- what is an affiliate/referral link

No disguised ads.
No fake recommendations.
No hidden ranking manipulation.

If a commercial relationship exists, it should be stated plainly.

---

## 7. Recommendation Standard

Citizen Smith should only recommend a partner or advertiser if all of the following are true:

1. the service is relevant to the user’s problem
2. the service is not exploitative
3. the service is understandable to low-confidence users
4. the service does not create additional harm or confusion
5. the recommendation would still be defensible if no money changed hands

If any of these fail, the recommendation should not be made.

---

## 8. Position on MSE-Style Economics

Citizen Smith accepts the MoneySavingExpert principle:
- a site can be genuinely helpful
- a site can be commercially successful
- those are not contradictory if the revenue model stays aligned with the user’s interests

The lesson is not “copy MSE’s ads.”
The lesson is:
- trust creates audience
- usefulness creates trust
- clean monetisation can sit on top of that without corrupting it

Citizen Smith should follow this principle in spirit:
**public help first, ethical commercial layer second.**

---

## 9. Decision Test for Any Future Revenue Opportunity

Before accepting any commercial arrangement, ask:

1. Would I still recommend this if we were not paid?
2. Could this harm a vulnerable user?
3. Could this confuse a low-confidence user?
4. Could this make our rights guidance look compromised?
5. Would I be comfortable explaining this relationship publicly?
6. Would I be comfortable placing this in front of someone in distress?

If the answer creates doubt, reject it.

---

## 10. Operating Principle

Citizen Smith should become known for one thing above all:

**It is on the citizen’s side first.**

If that remains true:
- trust will grow
- audience will grow
- aligned commercial opportunities will follow

If that stops being true, the whole model fails.
