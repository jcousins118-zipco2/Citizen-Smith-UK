# Citizen Smith — Approved Sources Register (V1: Debt & Credit)

## Purpose
This register defines the sources allowed for V1 debt and credit content.
Only these sources should be used for issue pages, templates, escalation guides, and evidence notes unless explicitly added later.

---

## Tier 1 — Primary / official authority

### 1. FCA Handbook
- **Organisation:** Financial Conduct Authority
- **Use for:** CONC rules, arrears handling, vulnerability, communications, collections, forbearance
- **Priority topics:**
  - CONC 7
  - CONC 8
  - consumer treatment / vulnerability-related guidance where relevant
- **Reason:** Core regulatory backbone for consumer credit firms and debt collection conduct

### 2. FCA official guidance / consumer pages
- **Organisation:** Financial Conduct Authority
- **Use for:** supporting interpretation, complaints, firm obligations, vulnerability framing
- **Reason:** official regulator explanation alongside handbook rules

### 3. GOV.UK
- **Organisation:** UK Government
- **Use for:** debt advice routes, breathing space overview, complaint processes, court / enforcement process where relevant
- **Reason:** official citizen-facing process information

### 4. legislation.gov.uk
- **Organisation:** UK Government
- **Use for:** statutory wording where an issue touches legislation rather than FCA rules alone
- **Examples:** harassment-related legislation, insolvency-related legislation, formal legal process
- **Reason:** primary legislative source

### 5. Financial Ombudsman Service (FOS)
- **Organisation:** Financial Ombudsman Service
- **Use for:** complaint escalation process, timing, complaint handling expectations, examples of redress framing
- **Reason:** official complaint escalation route for unresolved consumer credit disputes

### 6. Insolvency Service / Breathing Space official materials
- **Organisation:** UK Government / official scheme sources
- **Use for:** breathing space process, scope, protections, eligibility references
- **Reason:** official route for statutory breathing space references

---

## Tier 2 — Trusted support / practical guidance

### 7. Citizens Advice
- **Use for:** plain-English interpretation, complaint routes, debt rights, practical next steps, sample letters where available
- **Reason:** widely trusted, citizen-friendly, non-profit guidance

### 8. StepChange Debt Charity
- **Use for:** hardship, payment pause, breathing space, debt options, creditor negotiation framing
- **Reason:** specialist debt charity with practical consumer guidance

### 9. National Debtline
- **Use for:** debt collection conduct, template letters, enforcement explanation, consumer options
- **Reason:** strong debt-specific practical guidance and template support

### 10. MoneyHelper / Money and Pensions Service
- **Use for:** debt options overview, support routing, financial difficulty context
- **Reason:** official / semi-official support ecosystem reference

### 11. PayPlan
- **Use for:** practical debt process guidance only
- **Reason:** useful support material, but lower priority than Citizens Advice / StepChange / National Debtline
- **Restriction:** never use PayPlan to override Tier 1 or stronger Tier 2 sources

### 12. CAP UK
- **Use for:** practical debt support references only
- **Reason:** recognised support body, useful as supplementary guidance
- **Restriction:** do not use as primary authority for legal or regulatory interpretation

## Tier 3 — Citizen Smith internal outputs

These can be published only when backed by Tier 1 and/or Tier 2.

- issue summaries
- template letters
- escalation guides
- evidence logs
- case timeline tools
- contradiction / harassment / vulnerability workflows

## Approved V1 Issue Areas

Research and content creation should focus on these first:

1. **Two parties chasing the same debt**
   - MBNA / collector style dual-contact problem
   - likely source focus: FCA CONC communications / collections rules, FOS complaints process

2. **Default threat during active dispute**
   - source focus: FCA rules on dispute handling / enforcement pause, FOS complaint route

3. **Contradictory messages from the same creditor group**
   - source focus: FCA record accuracy / fair treatment / complaint handling

4. **Vulnerability and hardship being ignored**
   - source focus: FCA vulnerability guidance, CONC fair treatment / forbearance, StepChange / Citizens Advice practical framing

5. **Excessive calls, emails, or oppressive contact**
   - source focus: FCA contact conduct rules, National Debtline guidance, harassment-related legal sources where needed

6. **Breathing space / payment pause / time to pay**
   - source focus: GOV.UK, Insolvency Service, StepChange, National Debtline

7. **Complaint escalation to FOS**
   - source focus: FOS official pages, FCA complaint handling expectations

## Source Use Rules

### Always prefer in this order
1. FCA Handbook / legislation / official scheme rules
2. FOS / GOV.UK / official regulator explanation
3. Citizens Advice / StepChange / National Debtline
4. supplementary support sites
5. Citizen Smith summary

### Every published page must record
- source title
- organisation
- URL
- date checked
- issue supported
- whether it is law, regulator guidance, ombudsman process, or support guidance

### Every template must map back to
- one issue area
- one or more approved sources
- one escalation route

---

## Not Approved for V1 unless separately reviewed

- random legal blogs
- SEO legal content farms
- AI-generated legal pages with no source chain
- forum posts as authority
- Reddit as authority
- sales-led debt company content used as legal basis
- unverified social media threads

These may help discover leads, but may not be relied upon as authority.

---

## Working Method for Research Agents

When researching debt/credit topics, the output should be structured as:

1. **Issue name**
2. **Primary rule / regulation**
3. **Official process source**
4. **Trusted plain-English support source**
5. **Short approved quote(s)**
6. **Template implications**
7. **Escalation path**
8. **Date checked**

No bulk scraping. No full-page copying. No unsourced claims.

---

## V1 Principle

Debt & credit V1 should win on:
- clarity
- source quality
- practical usability
- strong escalation logic
- disciplined use of authority

Not on volume.
