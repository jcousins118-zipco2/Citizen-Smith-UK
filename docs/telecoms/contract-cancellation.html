Contract / Cancellation Dispute

Use when: the provider is refusing cancellation, charging high exit fees, claiming you are in a minimum term, or the contract terms seem unfair.

The likely position

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 (s.62), unfair contract terms are not binding on consumers. This includes excessive exit fees, automatic contract renewals, or terms that create a significant imbalance. Under Ofcom General Condition C1, the provider must treat you fairly. Under GC C7, they must give clear contract information at the point of sale — if important information was hidden, that may strengthen your position. Mid-contract price rises may also give you the right to leave without penalty.

What to keep

  • contract or sign-up documents
  • cancellation requests
  • price-rise notifications
  • early exit fee details

Template wording

If they keep going

Escalate to the Communications Ombudsman or CISAS. They can order the provider to release you from the contract or refund fees. Keep all contract documents and price-rise notifications.
