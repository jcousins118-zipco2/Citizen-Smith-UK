Late Notice / Old Bill

Use when: a bill has appeared after a long gap — sometimes weeks, months, or even years — often for a service you thought was cancelled or settled.

The likely position

Under Ofcom General Condition C1 (Fairness), providers must treat you fairly. A bill that arrives long after the service period — especially one that causes unfair surprise — may be challenged as unfair or improperly raised. The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008 also protect you from misleading or aggressive billing practices. If the provider took too long to bill you, and the delay was not your fault, this may be a strong ground for challenge.

What to keep

  • the bill or notification
  • your account history if available
  • previous communications with the provider
  • any date discrepancies

If they keep going

If the provider insists on payment, escalate to the Communications Ombudsman or CISAS. The Ombudsman will consider whether the delay in billing was fair — if it was not, they can cancel the charge.
