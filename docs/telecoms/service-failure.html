Service Failure / Poor Performance

Use when: your broadband, phone, or mobile service is down, slow, unreliable, or the provider is not meeting its commitments.

The likely position

Under the Ofcom Automatic Compensation Code (as of April 2026), you may be entitled to automatic compensation: £10.34 per day after 2 full working days without service (repair delay), £32.31 for a missed appointment, and £6.46 per day for a delayed service start. Providers that have signed up to the code must pay this without you having to claim it. Separately, under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 (s.49), the service must be provided with reasonable care and skill. If it is not, you may be entitled to a reduction in your monthly charge or the right to leave the contract early.

What to keep

  • outage logs or dates
  • reports to the provider
  • repair or missed-appointment records
  • compensation code entitlement

Template wording

If they keep going

Escalate to the Communications Ombudsman or CISAS. They can order the provider to pay compensation, reduce your bills, and release you from the contract if the service is consistently poor.
