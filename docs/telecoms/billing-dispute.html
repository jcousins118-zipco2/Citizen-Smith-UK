Telecoms Billing Dispute

Use when: your phone, broadband, or mobile bill is wrong, contains unexpected charges, or you believe you are being overcharged.

The likely position

Under Ofcom General Condition C1 (Fairness), providers must treat you fairly in how they bill and handle complaints. Under GC C5 (Complaint handling), they must have a proper complaints process and resolve issues promptly. Charges that are misleading or unfair may also breach the Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008. If the provider cannot resolve the issue, you can escalate to the Communications Ombudsman or CISAS.

What to keep

  • recent bills
  • cancellation confirmation if relevant
  • complaint history
  • any promotional pricing documents

Template wording

If they keep going

If the provider does not resolve the complaint within 8 weeks or sends a deadlock letter, escalate to the Communications Ombudsman (free) or CISAS (the alternative dispute scheme). Which one depends on the provider's membership.
