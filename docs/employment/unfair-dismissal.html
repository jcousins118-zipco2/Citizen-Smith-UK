Unfair Dismissal

Use when: you have been sacked and believe the reason was unfair, the process was wrong, or the real reason was discrimination, whistleblowing, or another protected factor.

The likely position

Under the Employment Rights Act 1996 (s.94), you have the right not to be unfairly dismissed. For a dismissal to be fair, the employer must show it was for one of five potentially fair reasons (conduct, capability, redundancy, illegality, SOSR) and that a fair procedure was followed (s.98). Certain reasons are automatically unfair regardless of service — including whistleblowing, pregnancy/maternity, trade union membership, health and safety, and asserting statutory rights. You normally need 2 years of continuous service for a standard unfair dismissal claim, unless the reason is automatically unfair.

What to keep

  • the dismissal letter
  • contract or employment details
  • evidence of the real reason
  • length of service

If they keep going

If the employer does not resolve the grievance, go to ACAS for early conciliation — this is a required step before an employment tribunal claim. Deadlines are tight: you have 3 months minus 1 day from the effective date of termination. Act quickly.
