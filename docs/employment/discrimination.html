Discrimination / Harassment at Work

Use when: you are being treated differently or harassed at work because of a protected characteristic (age, disability, race, sex, religion, sexual orientation, gender reassignment, pregnancy, marriage/civil partnership).

The likely position

Under the Equality Act 2010 (s.13 — direct discrimination, s.19 — indirect discrimination, s.26 — harassment, s.39 — employment protections), you are protected from discrimination and harassment in the workplace. There is no minimum service period needed for discrimination claims — you are protected from day one. Your employer has a duty to prevent discrimination and harassment. If they knew or should have known and did nothing, they are liable.

What to keep

  • dates and descriptions of incidents
  • emails, messages, or witness details
  • evidence of different treatment
  • any grievance already submitted

If they keep going

If the employer does not resolve the grievance, go to ACAS early conciliation then employment tribunal. Deadlines are 3 months minus 1 day from the last act of discrimination. A charity, trade union, or equality law adviser can help with the process.
