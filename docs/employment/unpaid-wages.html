Unpaid Wages / Holiday Pay

Use when: your employer has not paid you wages, holiday pay, notice pay, or the correct minimum wage.

The likely position

Under the Employment Rights Act 1996 (s.13), unauthorised deductions from wages are unlawful. Under the National Minimum Wage Act 1998, you must be paid at least the statutory minimum for your age. Under the Working Time Regulations 1998 (reg.14), most workers are entitled to 5.6 weeks of paid holiday per year and should be paid at the correct rate. Claims for unauthorised deductions must normally be brought within 3 months minus 1 day of the last deduction.

What to keep

  • payslips and contracts
  • bank statements
  • records of hours worked
  • any grievance or complaint already sent

If they keep going

If the employer does not pay, go to ACAS early conciliation then employment tribunal. HMRC can also investigate minimum wage and holiday pay issues — they can act on your behalf. Act quickly — the 3-month gap limit applies to most deduction claims.
