DWP Interview / Evidence Request

Use when: the DWP is asking you to attend an interview, assessment, or provide evidence and you need changes to make it possible for you.

The likely position

Under the Equality Act 2010 (s.20), the DWP has a duty to make reasonable adjustments for disabled people — this includes assessments, interviews, and communications. You are entitled to ask for changes such as home visits, longer appointment times, an accessible room, a support person to attend, or information in a different format. If the DWP refuses a reasonable adjustment without good reason, that may itself be a disability discrimination matter.

What to keep

  • the interview or evidence request letter
  • notes about barriers or access needs
  • medical evidence if relevant
  • any existing reasonable adjustments

If they keep going

If the DWP refuses reasonable adjustments, make a formal complaint. An MP referral or disability charity support can help. Refusal without good reason may be a disability discrimination issue — consider legal advice.
