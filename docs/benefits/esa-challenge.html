ESA Refusal or Cut Challenge

Use when: ESA has been refused, reduced, or you have been placed in the wrong group (Support Group vs WRAG) and you believe the decision is wrong.

The likely position

Under the Welfare Reform Act 2012 (s.102), you may be able to request a mandatory reconsideration. The ESA Regulations 2008 (reg.30) govern Limited Capability for Work (SC019 — standard rate) and reg.35 governs the Support Group (SC020 — higher rate). If the WCA assessment placed you in the wrong group or you were refused entirely when you meet the criteria, the decision may be challenged. You normally need to request reconsideration within 1 month of the decision date.

What to keep

  • the ESA decision letter
  • health or medical evidence
  • daily notes about condition and work limits
  • the WCA report if available

If they keep going

If mandatory reconsideration is refused, appeal to the First-tier Tribunal (free, independent). About 70% of ESA appeals succeed. Get specialist help before the tribunal — Citizens Advice or a law centre can prepare the case.
