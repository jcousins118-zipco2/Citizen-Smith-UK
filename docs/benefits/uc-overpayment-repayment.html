UC Overpayment / Repayment Challenge

Use when: the DWP says you have been overpaid Universal Credit and you believe the amount is wrong, the overpayment was their fault, or the repayment rate is too high for your situation.

The likely position

Under the Universal Credit Regulations 2013 (reg.71-72), overpayments are recoverable from the claimant in most cases, but you may be able to challenge the amount, the recovery rate, or argue it was a DWP or official error. If the overpayment was caused by a DWP mistake (official error), the amount may still be recoverable, but the circumstances matter — including whether you reasonably believed the payments were correct. You can also ask for the repayment rate to be lowered if it is causing hardship.

What to keep

  • the overpayment decision letter
  • UC payment history
  • hardship notes if repayment rate is too high
  • any evidence of DWP error

If they keep going

If the DWP refuses to change the decision or the repayment rate, you can ask for a mandatory reconsideration and then appeal to the First-tier Tribunal. You can also request hardship protection through your UC journal.
