Prove Authority / Chain-of-Title Request

Use when: more than one collector appears to be involved, or you do not know who actually owns or controls the debt.

The likely position

You may be able to require the firm to explain who owns the debt, who has authority to act, and why more than one collector may be involved.

What to keep

  • all collector letters or emails
  • different balances or account numbers
  • payment instructions from each party

Template wording

If they keep going

Formal complaint first, then Financial Ombudsman Service if the authority or control position remains unclear.
