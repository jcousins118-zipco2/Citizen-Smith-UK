Unaffordable Lending Challenge

Use when: you believe the lender should never have given you this credit in the first place.

The likely position

You may be able to challenge the lending decision if the credit was unaffordable and the lender failed to assess it properly.

What to keep

  • income and outgoings at the time
  • repeat borrowing pattern if relevant
  • statements showing unsustainable use

Template wording

If they keep going

Formal complaint first, then Financial Ombudsman Service if the response is inadequate.
