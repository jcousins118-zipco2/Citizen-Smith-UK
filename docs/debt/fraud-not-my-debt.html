Fraud / Not-My-Debt Notice

Use when: the debt was created by fraud, impersonation, or an account you do not recognise.

The likely position

You may be able to require the firm to stop collection, investigate the fraud properly, and correct any damaged records.

What to keep

  • fraud report reference, if you have one
  • credit file entries
  • letters or emails about the account
  • anything showing the account details are wrong

Template wording

If they keep going

Formal complaint, then Financial Ombudsman Service. If records remain wrong, keep the route open for credit-file correction.
