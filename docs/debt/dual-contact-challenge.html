Dual-Contact Challenge

Use when: a creditor and debt collector are both contacting you about the same debt.

Likely rule

FCA CONC 7.13.10 — you should not be subjected to overlapping or repetitive contact by multiple parties about the same debt.

What to keep

  • emails or letters from both parties
  • different balances or payment instructions
  • dates and times of overlapping contact
  • reference numbers from both organisations

Template wording

Next escalation

Formal complaint first, then Financial Ombudsman Service if the confusion or harm continues.
