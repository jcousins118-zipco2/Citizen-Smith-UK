Unfair Fees Challenge

Use when: you are being hit with repeated charges, late fees, or over-limit fees that seem unfair or excessive.

The likely position

You may be able to challenge fees that are disproportionate, unfair, or making financial difficulty worse.

What to keep

  • statements showing the charges
  • the running total of fees
  • any complaint or hardship history linked to the account

Template wording

If they keep going

Formal complaint first, then Financial Ombudsman Service if the issue is not resolved fairly.
