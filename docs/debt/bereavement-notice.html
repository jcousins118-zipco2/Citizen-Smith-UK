Bereavement Notice

Use when: a creditor is dealing badly with a bereaved family member, executor, or next of kin.

The likely position

You may be able to require the firm to stop inappropriate contact, move the case into its bereavement process, and handle the matter more carefully.

What to keep

  • letters or emails sent after notification of death
  • the dates you informed them
  • any estate or reference details if relevant

Template wording

If they keep going

Formal complaint first, then Financial Ombudsman Service if the handling remains poor or distressing.
