Invalid Default Notice Challenge

Use when: you believe the default notice is defective, incomplete, or does not comply with the proper requirements.

The likely position

You may be able to challenge reliance on a default notice if it is defective or does not meet the proper legal requirements.

What to keep

  • the full default notice
  • dates it was sent and received if known
  • related statements or correspondence

Template wording

If they keep going

Formal complaint first, then Financial Ombudsman Service. Keep the full notice and timeline carefully.
