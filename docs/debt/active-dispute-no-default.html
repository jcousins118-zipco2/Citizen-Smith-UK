Active Dispute / No-Default Warning

Use when: you already disputed the debt, but they are threatening default, closure, or enforcement anyway.

Likely rule

FCA CONC 7.14.1 — enforcement should not continue while a genuine dispute remains unresolved.

What to keep

  • your first dispute email or letter
  • any complaint reference
  • later threat messages
  • a timeline of unanswered correspondence

Template wording

Next escalation

Formal complaint, then FOS. If harm happens later, keep a route open for credit-file correction.
