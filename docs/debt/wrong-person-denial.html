Wrong-Person Denial

Use when: the debt is being chased against the wrong person, or your records appear to be mixed up with someone else’s.

The likely position

You may be able to require the firm to verify the identity properly, stop wrongful pursuit, and correct inaccurate records.

What to keep

  • letters or emails using incorrect details
  • address history if relevant
  • credit file mismatch evidence

Template wording

If they keep going

Formal complaint, then Financial Ombudsman Service. If the record stays wrong, keep the route open for data and credit-file correction.
