Repair Request

Use when: repairs are needed (leaks, damage, structural issues, electrical faults, etc.) and the landlord is not responding.

The likely position

Under the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 (s.11), your landlord must keep in repair the structure and exterior of the property, and the installations for water, gas, electricity, sanitation, and heating. Under the Homes (Fitness for Human Habitation) Act 2018, the property must be fit for human habitation throughout the tenancy. If the problem is serious enough, it can be enforced by the council under the HHSRS (Housing Act 2004).

What to keep

  • photos or videos of the problem
  • dates you first reported it
  • messages with the landlord or agent
  • any health or safety impact

Template wording

If they keep going

Formal complaint through the landlord's process, then council environmental health (for HHSRS enforcement) or Housing Ombudsman (for social landlords). In serious cases the council can issue notices and carry out work.
