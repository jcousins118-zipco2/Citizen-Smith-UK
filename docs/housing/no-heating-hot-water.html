No Heating or Hot Water

Use when: the property has no heating, no hot water, or an unsafe heating system and the landlord is not fixing it urgently.

The likely position

Under the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 (s.11), your landlord must keep the installation for space heating and hot water in proper working order. This is an urgent repair — the landlord must act quickly. If they do not, the council environmental health team can issue emergency repair notices under the Housing Act 2004 (HHSRS, where lack of heating classified as a Category 1 hazard in cold weather).

What to keep

  • when the failure started
  • every report made (dates, times, how)
  • any health or child-safety impact
  • weather conditions if relevant

Template wording

If they keep going

Contact the council environmental health team — they can issue emergency notices. Housing Ombudsman route for social landlords. If the problem continues, the council can carry out the work and charge the landlord.
