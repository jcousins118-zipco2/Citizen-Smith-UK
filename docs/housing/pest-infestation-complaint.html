Pest Infestation Complaint

Use when: pests (rats, mice, cockroaches, bedbugs, etc.) are linked to the condition of the property or the landlord's failure to maintain it.

The likely position

If pest infestation is caused by or linked to disrepair (holes in walls, broken drains, structural gaps), the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 (s.11) applies — the landlord must keep the structure and exterior in repair, which includes sealing entry points. Pest infestation can be classified as a Category 1 hazard under the HHSRS (Housing Act 2004) where it poses a health risk. If the problem is caused by the tenant's behaviour (e.g. leaving food out), the position is different — but many infestations have a structural cause.

What to keep

  • photos or videos
  • where the pests are coming from if known
  • dates reported
  • health or hygiene impacts

Template wording

If they keep going

Formal complaint through the landlord's process, then council environmental health. If the council confirms the infestation is linked to disrepair, they can take enforcement action. Housing Ombudsman for social landlords.
