Disrepair Escalation

Use when: you have already reported the problem and the landlord has not taken action.

The likely position

If the landlord has failed to act after you reported the problem, you may be able to escalate to the council environmental health team for a formal inspection under the Housing Health and Safety Rating System (HHSRS) — created by the Housing Act 2004. If the problem is a Category 1 hazard, the council must take enforcement action. For social landlords, the Housing Ombudsman can also investigate complaints about failure to repair. The Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 (s.11) remains the underlying repair duty.

What to keep

  • timeline of all reports
  • photos and videos
  • responses or non-responses
  • any worsening damage or health impact

Template wording

If they keep going

Contact council environmental health for a HHSRS inspection. Housing Ombudsman for social landlords. If the council issues an improvement notice and it is ignored, the council can carry out work and recover costs from the landlord.
