Prepayment Meter Challenge

Use when: the supplier is threatening to install a prepayment meter (by warrant or remote switch) and you believe it is unsafe, unfair, or you are in a vulnerable situation.

The likely position

Under Ofgem SLC 27.5, prepayment meters can only be installed where it is "safe and reasonably practicable." Forced installation for very vulnerable customers is prohibited. The supplier must assess vulnerability before any warrant or remote switch action. Under SLC 27A.5, customers on a prepayment meter who are self-disconnecting or self-rationing because of financial difficulty may be entitled to Additional Support Credit. If you are on the Priority Services Register (PSR), your protections are stronger.

What to keep

  • threat letters or texts
  • vulnerability or health notes
  • payment offers already made
  • Priority Services Register status

Template wording

If they keep going

Formal complaint immediately. If the supplier proceeds regardless, escalate to the Energy Ombudsman. Ofgem can also investigate systemic breaches. Check your PSR status and ask to be added if you are not already.
