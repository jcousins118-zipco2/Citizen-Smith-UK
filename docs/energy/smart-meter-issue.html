Smart Meter Issue

Use when: your smart meter is not working properly, not sending readings, or the supplier is relying on inaccurate estimated bills as a result.

The likely position

Under Ofgem SLC 24 (Metering), the supplier is responsible for ensuring the meter works properly. If the smart meter fails and estimated bills result, the supplier should investigate and correct the billing. Under SLC 40, bills must be accurate. Estimated billing for extended periods due to a faulty smart meter may not meet this standard. You may also be entitled to compensation if the supplier failed to fix the issue within a reasonable time.

What to keep

  • manual meter readings
  • photos of the meter
  • estimated bills
  • fault reports to the supplier

Template wording

If they keep going

If unresolved after 8 weeks or deadlock, refer to the Energy Ombudsman. Faulty smart meters and resulting billing errors are routine grounds for ombudsman correction and compensation.
