Direct Debit / Credit Refund

Use when: the supplier is taking too much by direct debit, refusing to refund a credit balance, or setting the monthly amount based on inaccurate estimates.

The likely position

Under the Standards of Conduct (Ofgem SLC 0), your supplier must treat you fairly. Direct debit amounts should reflect actual usage — not an inflated estimate designed to build a large credit balance. If your account is in significant credit and you want the money back, the supplier should refund it promptly. Under SLC 31G, the supplier must tell you about your right to refer a dispute to the Energy Ombudsman.

What to keep

  • direct debit amounts
  • balance screenshots or bills
  • meter readings
  • refund requests already made

Template wording

If they keep going

If the supplier refuses to review the direct debit or refund the credit, escalate to the Energy Ombudsman after 8 weeks or deadlock. The Ombudsman frequently orders refunds of unreasonable credit balances.
