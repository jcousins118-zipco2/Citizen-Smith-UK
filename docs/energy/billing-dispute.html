Energy Billing Dispute

Use when: your energy bill is wrong, contains charges you do not recognise, or the supplier is not handling billing fairly.

The likely position

Under Ofgem Standard Licence Condition 21BA (Backbilling), your supplier cannot bill you for energy used more than 12 months ago if you were not accurately billed at the time and were not at fault. Under the Standards of Conduct (SLC 0), the supplier must treat you fairly and handle complaints properly. If the bill is genuinely wrong, you may be able to dispute it under the supplier's own complaints process and escalate to the Energy Ombudsman.

What to keep

  • recent bills
  • meter readings or photos
  • payment history
  • dates of any disputed charges

If they keep going

If the supplier does not resolve the complaint within 8 weeks or sends a deadlock letter, refer the case to the Energy Ombudsman (free service). They can investigate and order the supplier to correct the billing.
